Why the swap

Your editor already works this way

Conductor and Superset, the agent workspaces on your Mac, use the same three-part shape: the list of work on the left, the agent in the middle, and that one workspace's changes, checks and PR on the right.

LeftEvery workspace or session, grouped by status (needs input, running)
MiddleThe agent's terminal or chat
RightThis workspace's diff, checks and Create PR

So the left side gets the project and everything in it, and the right side gets one session in full, including its PRs. The right side follows the tab you're on, the same way Active Terminal does today.

I couldn't screenshot Superset or Cursor: adding them to the computer allowlist was blocked. The pattern above comes from their public docs.
